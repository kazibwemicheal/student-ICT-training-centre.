About Us

The ISBAT University Student ICT Training Centre is a hands-on learning space where students build practical technology skills alongside their academic studies. Our modern computer labs, reliable internet and up-to-date software let learners practise real tasks instead of only reading about them.

Our instructors are experienced industry practitioners who guide students through short, focused programmes. Whether you are starting out or sharpening existing skills, the centre helps you gain confidence, build a portfolio and prepare for the workplace.
